Our story

Thikana Estates started with a simple idea: finding a home should not mean ten phone calls to learn one price. So every listing here shows its price per marla, its size in marla and square feet, and how that price compares with similar homes.

We check the homes we mark as Verified, we take down homes the moment they are sold, and we never share your phone number. Your location stays on your own phone or computer.

Good to know

Is my location shared?

No. Your position is worked out in your own browser, rounded, and kept there. It is never sent to us or written in any sheet.

Where are my saved homes kept?

Only in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser data they are gone, so use the same device when you come back.

How is a price insight worked out?

We compare the price per marla with the middle price of similar listings (same purpose and type, same area when there are enough). It is a guide, not a valuation.

Do you charge to list my property?

This is a demo website, so there are no real fees. A real agency would explain its commission clearly before you sign anything.
